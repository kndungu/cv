\lettersection{How it started}
In the second year of my Biochemistry undergraduate studies, MIT and Harvard partnered to launch edx; an online platform through which anyone could take some of their courses for free. I was obsessed with MIT growing up and jumped at the opportunity to take one of their courses, just to see if I could. That course was 6.00x: Introduction to Computer Science and Programming and it was one of the hardest things I had ever done, I was instantly hooked. Afterwards I took Harvard's version of the same course and later multiple programming courses on edx, Coursera, Udacity and Udemy.

\lettersection{Early Career}
While in my final year of university Andela launched in Kenya with a unique proposition, they'd teach you how to code then place you in jobs at top US companies. It was too good an opportunity to pass up. At Andela I found structure, I was no longer learning anything and everything that piqued my interest but rather the most in demand skills, industry best practices, and it gave me an opportunity to practice my soft skills. Though my initial placements were short lived, I got valuable experience that was instrumental to my success at future placements.

\lettersection{Finding a rhythm}
Eventually I got to work with some truly exceptional managers and teammates who provided not only a challenging environment that promoted growth, but also a safe space where one could fail and learn. Here my skills and responsibilities grew until I became key member of the team.

\lettersection{Now}
Having gone through Cloudflare’s values and capabilities, I’m confident I’ve found a company whose values align with mine and that is worth considering a job shift for. I believe myself to be a good cultural and technical fit and am willing to validate this through your interview process if I am selected.

\end{cvletter}
